Experiments Overview

This hub outlines the progression of experiments in Echo, exploring communication-efficient cooperative driving across eight sequential milestones.

Each experiment below has a dedicated page with detailed setup, world-model loss curves, request-policy graphs, and playable driving videos.

Detailed Experiment Sub-Sections

Experiment 1: Baseline Integration
Initial proof-of-concept pipeline, 26k dataset, 3-step horizon selection, and baseline video.

Experiment 2: Data Scaling & Intent Previews
Scaling to 80k+ transitions and querying non-mutating vehicle intent previews.

Experiment 3: Spatial Realignment & Baselines
Binding messages to 8 fixed spatial slots around the ego and producing baseline checkpoints.

Experiment 4: Crash Forensics & Safety Guards
Root cause analysis of planner failures and executive safety guards eliminating crashes (97% → 0%).

Experiment 5: Matched-Intent Challenge Scenarios
Parallel stay vs. cut-in worlds proving that intent messages cut crashes in half (12 → 6).

Experiment 6: Selective Request Learning
A training-only counterfactual teacher penalizes questions whose replies do not change the planner's move.

Experiment 7: Oracle-Taught Requests
A privileged simulation oracle teaches the Request DQN via behavior cloning, slashing cut-in crashes to 3.75%.

Experiment 8: Double-DQN Fine-Tuning
A standalone feasibility run using online action selection and target-network action evaluation.

Current Recommendations

  • Best Learned Cooperative Policy: Experiment 7 (checkpoints/experiment_7/RequestDQN/best.pt) with --safety-guard --max-target-speed 30 --lane-change-guard.
  • Highway Cruising Baseline: On open highways with predictable IDM traffic, Ask Nobody achieves zero communication overhead and collision-free driving.
  • Why Communication Matters: In high-speed, ambiguous cut-in scenes, intent messages are the only way to distinguish benign straight-driving cars from aggressive cut-in vehicles, reducing crashes from ~9% down to 3.75%.
