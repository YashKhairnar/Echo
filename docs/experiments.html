Experiments Overview

This hub outlines the progression of experiments in Echo, exploring communication-efficient cooperative driving across six sequential milestones.

Each experiment below has a dedicated page with detailed setup, world-model loss curves, request-policy graphs, and playable driving videos.

Experiment Progression at a Glance

ExperimentFocus AreaDatasetModel ArchitectureKey Outcome / Finding
Experiment 1Baseline Proof-of-Concept26k transitions116 in / 55 outProved basic integration of HighwayEnv, MLPx, and Request DQN. High crash rates.
Experiment 2Scaling & Intent Previews80k+ transitions116 in / 55 outAdded neighbor next-action previews. Established data collection pipelines.
Experiment 3Spatial Slot Realignment263k transitions116 in / 55 outFixed spatial alignment for neighbor messages. Produced baseline checkpoints (WM3/DQN3).
Experiment 4Planner Crash Diagnosis & Safety Guards566k transitions116 in / 55 outIdentified runaway speed (50 m/s) and missing controller state. Safety guards reduced crashes from 97% to 0%.
Experiment 5Matched-Intent Scenarios & Mixed Traffic145k transitions118 in / 57 outProved value of communication in cut-in scenes: intent messages cut crashes in half (12 → 6).
Experiment 6Selective Requests + Smooth ControlReused Experiment 5Frozen WM5 + selective DQNBest learned configuration: fewer requests, 9/200 intent crashes, and 240 completed lane changes; ask-nobody still leads adjusted return.

Current recommendation

  • Best learned/safety-oriented policy: Experiment 6 DQN with --comfort-ttc 6.
  • Best communication-cost-adjusted baseline: ask nobody.
  • Why the learned policy is still useful: it reduces intent crashes from 14/200 to 9/200 and produces substantially more smooth, completed lane changes, but its remaining requests still cost more reward than they add.
